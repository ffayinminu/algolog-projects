## 2. Content

Per **course**:
- Final or near-final video files
- Title, description, category (Leadership, Integrity, Business Ethics, etc.)
- Segment/chapter breakdown
- Reflection / discussion questions per segment
- Downloadable resources (PDF guides, scripture references)

Per **speaker / leader**:
- Name, title, short bio, high-resolution headshot, associated courses, brief faith journey/testimony

**Articles / Fresh Perspectives & Power Reads:** text, images, categories
**Faithful Success Stories:** testimonials — name, role, quote, optional photo
**About Us:** vision, mission, guiding scriptures, narrative
**Events & Speakers:** any launch-time events to feature
**Pricing inputs:** individual monthly/yearly amounts; organization tier structure + seat pricing; what the free tier includes
