## 2. The 4 lessons worth stealing

Each lesson = **Principle → Why it works → Our move.**

**1. Best-fit before build (his biggest emphasis)**
- Principle: he spends most of the effort *before* the ad exists — finding the best-fit audience and confirming the offer actually fits that buyer. Research the audience, sharpen the offer, and fix the website first. "Marketing isn't expensive — poor structure is."
- Why: ad spend on the wrong audience or a broken funnel just buys clicks that never convert.
- Our move: a **pre-ad fit gate** — no spend until we've defined the best-fit audience, confirmed offer↔buyer fit, and have ICP + offer + working landing page in place.

**2. Buyer clarity over content volume**
- Principle: posting more isn't growth. Answer three questions: (1) Who exactly is the buyer? (2) What problem are they *actively* trying to solve? (3) Are people in your DM paying — or just being entertained?
- Why: content that ignores these gets likes, not sales.
- Our move: every campaign brief must answer those 3 questions before creative is made.

**3. Target intent, not just demographics**
- Principle: reach people "willing and ready to buy now," not merely relevant ones. The core failure is reaching the *wrong* audience.
- Why: intent beats reach — readiness converts.
- Our move: bias toward bottom-funnel + retargeting + conversion-optimized audiences.

**4. Lead with proof, close in the DM**
- Principle: show a real result (client on camera in her shop) and drive straight to DM — "No long talk, send a DM."
- Why: tangible proof + low-friction reply converts strangers faster than promises.
- Our move: run a **testimonial-capture routine** after every win; route ad CTAs to WhatsApp/DM with a fast-response SLA.

**Bonus — the wedge offer:** NavaTech sells **ad-account recovery & verification** (restricted/disabled accounts) done-for-you. It's a real Nigerian-advertiser pain point and a low-resistance way to start a relationship. Consider offering/partnering on it.

## 4. Paid ads baseline checklist — never run below this (2026 Meta)

- [ ] Pixel **+ Conversions API (CAPI)** installed and verified (no signal = wasted spend)
- [ ] Broad / **Advantage+** audiences, with the **creative doing the targeting**
- [ ] Hook + offer land in the **first 3 seconds**
- [ ] **Retargeting ladder** live: video viewers > engagers > site visitors > add-to-cart > purchasers
- [ ] Existing customers **excluded** from prospecting
- [ ] Consolidated campaign structure (CBO / Advantage+ budget), few ad sets, many creatives
- [ ] Respect the **learning phase** (~50 conversions/ad set/week; don't edit mid-learning)

## 5. Reuse this — operator teardown method

Run this on any operator we want to learn from. One pass = one filled capture sheet.

**Gather:** 5–10 result/testimonial reels · 3–5 teaching reels · bio + link-in-bio funnel · pricing tells · comment patterns.
**For each reel log:** hook (first line) · claimed result · proof shown · offer · CTA · funnel step.

**Blank capture sheet (copy per operator):**
- [ ] Offer — core promise, timeframe, price tells
- [ ] Targeting claims — audience type, interest stacks/exclusions, budget/scaling, pixel/CAPI
- [ ] Creative — hook formulas, format, proof style
- [ ] Funnel — path, response channel, speed-to-lead
- [ ] Niches served
- [ ] What's their real edge (and what they never reveal)

## Appendix A — What we found in NavaTech's reels (evidence)

**Offer:** get the business "sold out" — reach ready-to-buy buyers, convert visibility into sales. Done-for-you. Testimonial claims results in **under 24 hours** from a **5-day boosted** campaign. Price not public (DM for pricing).

**Targeting (their actual claims):** "set up a good target audience" that "pushes to the right people" / buyers "ready to buy immediately." Uses "audience data, content performance, and website behaviours." **No** interest stacks, budgets, campaign structure, Advantage+/CBO, or pixel/CAPI specifics disclosed.

**Creative:** problem-led hooks ("most businesses lose money on ads because of poor planning"). Founder talking-head + client talking-head in her store; bold on-screen captions. Proof is qualitative (more followers, a store walk-in), not dashboards.

**Funnel:** reel → DM (@kingnavatech) → close. Immediacy pushed ("send us a DM now").

**Niche:** generalist SMBs; strongest proof = fashion/clothing retail with a physical store.

**Honest gap:** all 9 reels are positioning/sales content, **not** a tactical media-buying teardown. The transferable value is the *packaging, foundation, intent, and proof* — not a hidden setting. For real tactics, watch any future *teaching* reels he posts.
